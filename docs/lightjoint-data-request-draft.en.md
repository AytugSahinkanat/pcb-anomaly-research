# LightJoint-D dataset request draft

**To:** Corresponding author of Zhang et al. (2023), *Printed circuit board solder joint quality inspection based on lightweight classification network*

**Subject:** Request for LightJoint-D PCB solder-joint dataset and source-board metadata

Dear Professor Zhou,

I am working on an academic research project on visual inspection of PCB components and solder-joint defects. We read your 2023 LightJoint-D paper, which reports 1,804 solder-joint images collected from 93 PCB images and states that the data are available from the corresponding author upon request.

Would you be able to share the dataset for non-commercial academic research? To evaluate methods without mixing crops from the same board across training and test sets, we would especially need:

- The 1,804 labeled solder-joint crops and their class labels.
- The original PCB images, or at least a source-board ID for every crop.
- Any existing train/validation/test split and the exact dataset version/counts used for the paper.
- The provenance of the 93 PCB images and which samples came from the two external datasets mentioned in the paper.
- The applicable data-use license or written terms for research use and publication of aggregate results.

If original board images cannot be shared, source-board IDs and confirmation that each crop belongs to one of the 93 boards would still help us design a leakage-aware evaluation. We would cite the paper and dataset as requested, and we would not redistribute the images.

Thank you for your time and for making this research resource available to the community.
